Field Manual

Win by reducing the enemy's Secrecy from 20 to 0, or by starting your turn with 7 groups in play.

Fronts: once per turn drag any card onto your Fronts pile (left). Each Front gives 1 Influence every turn.

Play a card by dragging it onto the field and paying its cost in Influence.

Act: click one of your ready groups (green glow), then Strike an enemy group or the enemy conspiracy, or attempt a Takeover: pay the target's cost as a bribe and roll 2d6 ≤ 5 + your Power − its Resistance. Spend 2 more Influence for each +1.

The Narrative: its holder draws an extra card each turn. Strike the holder's conspiracy to seize it.

Countermeasures are set face-down and spring on the enemy's move. Press L for the log, R for a new game. Full rules: RULES.md.
